Direction 03 pushed further: the progress ring around the play button, ten variations.

01

Time inside the ring

The ring holds the play button and, in small tabular digits under it, the time. Nothing else needs a clock. The rest in one row with a word under each.

Two rings

The outer ring is the song, the inner thinner ring is the line being sung: it fills and empties with every lyric line, so the panel breathes with the words.

A tick for every line
