hyperfeeling · bottom panel

Ten ways to lay out the player strip. All keep the red line-icon language; what changes is grouping, hierarchy and how much is shown at once.

01

Centred cluster

One progress line across the top. Play big in the dead centre with the line-steps either side. Everything else sits far right in a quiet group, time far left. Nothing floats alone.

02

Three zones, named

Left: the song (name, time). Centre: transport. Right: the view. Thin dividers and a tiny label over each zone, so the panel reads like a console, not a scatter.

The ring

04

Timeline first

A thick blood line with a tick for every lyric line; the tick you are on glows. Play at its left end, the time at its right. The rest hides behind one ⋯ until needed.

05

The capsule

A single floating capsule, centred: five things only (line back, play, line forward, lyrics, sound). Fullscreen and about live in the top bar. The progress is a hairline on the capsule’s top edge.

06

The record

Like a player app: a small turning disc with the song name and time at the left, transport centred, view controls right. Familiar at a glance.

The playhead is the button

No icons at all: small red serif words — prev · play · next · lyrics · sound · full — with the time. Editorial, matches the lyric type, and nothing needs decoding.
