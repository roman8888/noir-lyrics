[Intro — sparse upright bass, distant Rhodes, intimate smoky male baritone, almost whispered]
When God wants to see you rise,
He takes your comfort, hears your cries, by surprise.
He lets you fall before you fly,
So you can learn the reason why.
And when the light begins to fade,
You watch the dreams you built decay.
You search for signs in endless rain,
While every prayer returns as pain.

[Verse 1 — gentle noir jazz groove, brushed drums, restrained baritone]
He tests your patience, day by day,
And takes the ground you knew away.
He leaves you lost without a sign,
And makes you question His design.
You call for help, but no one hears,
You're left alone to face your fears.
You wonder why you're left alone,
Not knowing strength is born when you're on your own.

[Rap 1 — rapid rhythmic spoken-word, deep trip-hop beat, tight syncopated delivery, controlled anger]
They watched me fall and turned their backs,
Then blamed me for the wounds and cracks.
I gave them all, they watched me bleed,
Then turned away when I was in need.
I held them up when they were down,
But when I fell, they watched me drown.
They called me family, called me friend,
But all their love was just pretend.
I saw the truth behind their eyes,
Their sweetest words were dressed in lies.
I gave them chances, one by one,
But when I needed them, not one.
I won't forgive what they became,
They broke my trust, then cursed my name.
They thought my silence meant defeat,
But I'm still standing on my feet.

[Pre-Chorus — slower melodic delivery, piano opens, muted trumpet, gradual lift]
And in the silence, I heard You call,
You never left me through it all.
I thought the road had led nowhere,
But You were walking with me there.
You took the things I held so tight,
To teach me how to find my light.
And though I walk through endless rain,
I know the sun will rise again.

[Chorus — melancholic but hopeful, melodic baritone, warm strings, soulful saxophone]
I'm still here, though I've lost it all,
But I still rise each time I fall.
I lost the life I thought I'd know,
But found a strength I didn't know.
Some nights I still forget to breathe,
Still holding on to what I believe.
I haven't reached the end of me,
There's still a man I'm meant to be.

[Verse 2 — stripped-back noir jazz, weary conversational vocal, soft Rhodes]
I learned that kindness has a cost,
And counted everything I lost.
I gave my best to those I knew,
But none would see what I went through.
I stopped expecting them to care,
Or turn around and find them there.
Some bridges burn without a flame,
And some deserve to bear the shame.

[Rap 2 — faster rhythmic spoken-word, heavy bass, sharp percussive phrasing, defiant but never shouted]
They thought I'd crawl, they thought I'd break,
They never knew how much I'd take.
They saw the scars, ignored the fight,
They never saw the sleepless nights.
They thought my tears would make me weak,
But pain gave strength to every word I'd speak.
They shut me out, they slammed the door,
But I won't beg them anymore.
I gave them mercy, they gave me hell,
And left me with a tale to tell.
They tried to silence every word,
But now my voice demands to be heard.
I paid the price, I broke the chain,
And learned to live despite the pain.
I won't look back, I won't retreat,
My heart still breaks, but still it beats.

[Bridge — fragile near-whispered baritone, solitary piano, distant mournful saxophone, gradual warmth]
Maybe You broke what I called home,
To show me I was never home.
I searched for reasons in the rain,
And wondered what was left to gain.
Perhaps You never turned away,
You simply let me find my way.
I still don't know what lies ahead,
But I still hear the words You said.

[Final Chorus — emotional cinematic swell, full piano, warm strings, deep trip-hop drums, soulful saxophone]
I may be broken, but I'm here,
And somehow, hope is drawing near.
I cannot change the years I've lost,
Or take back all the lines they crossed.
They took so much, but not my soul,
I'm still the one who makes me whole.
The road is long, the night is deep,
And still, I rise when others sleep.

[Outro — instruments gradually disappear, intimate exhausted baritone, solitary piano, final line almost spoken]
And when the morning finds my face,
I'll find the strength to claim my place.
And though some wounds will always stay,
I'll carry them, but walk away.
And if You hear me when I pray,
Was losing all the only way?
I'm not the man I used to be,
I am the strength that set me free.

[End — final piano chord, long natural decay, silence]
